ABOUT THE PROJECT

A more accessible way to search.

VoiceBrowser combines your browser's Web Speech API with familiar web search, making it quick to find information without typing.

How it works

  1. Open the Speak page and press the microphone button.
  2. Allow microphone access when your browser asks.
  3. Say your search phrase. You will be taken to the results page.
  4. Use the Listen page to have the saved search phrase read back to you.
